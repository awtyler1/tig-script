When a lead arrives
Do this, in order
1
Your phone alerts you immediately
Push notification and text with name, number, age, and zip. Open Lead Connector.
2
Call within 5 minutes
Every minute after reduces your chance of reaching them. Do not wait.
3
No answer? Tap Attempted Contact
Select it on the disposition screen. The system handles follow-up automatically.
4
Reach them? Work the call script
Open the script before you dial. Know it cold. Improvisation is for after you have done this a hundred times.
5
Tap a disposition after every call
The app prompts you. Tap the outcome and the stage moves automatically.
i
Good to know: On mobile, tapping a disposition after a call moves the pipeline stage automatically. If something ever looks off, just let Austin know and it is an easy fix.
Pipeline reference
The funnel, stage by stage

Active stages move left to right. Two stages sit off to the side: Nurture for "not ready yet" and DNC for "do not contact." Dispositioning correctly keeps the whole system working.

New Lead
Auto-assigned. Do not move manually.
Attempted Contact
Called. No answer or voicemail.
Follow Up Scheduled
Spoke. Callback or next step agreed.
Appointment Set
Review on the calendar.
Enrolled
Application submitted.
Off-ramps
Nurture Not ready or not eligible yet. Parked for re-engagement.
DNC / Do Not Contact Do not call. No exceptions. Moving here is permanent.
